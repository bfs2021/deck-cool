The README gains recipes for setups that are CSS or a little of your own code, not options: centring the first and last slide without `loop`, highlighting the middle slide in view (centred, by `data-focal`, or at the start, by `--deck-slide-progress`), sizing a curve (a radius in pixels to `--deck-curve-radius` in slides, and the room the arc needs in the viewport), and custom controls and a counter built on `Deck.useDeck()`. They are linked from a Recipes index and from the sections they extend.
