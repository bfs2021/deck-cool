# Recipes

Common setups that are CSS or a little of your own code, not options. Each
runs in a story in this repo's storybook, named at the end of the recipe. In
the CSS, `.products` and `.showcase` are classes on `Deck.Root`.

- [Centre the first and last slide](/docs/recipes/centred-ends/)
- [Highlight the middle slide in view](/docs/recipes/middle-slide/)
- [Size a curve](/docs/recipes/size-a-curve/)
- [Custom controls and a counter](/docs/recipes/custom-controls/)
- [Change `effect`, `loop` or `autoplay` per breakpoint](/docs/recipes/per-breakpoint/)
- [Play a playdeck video in the focal slide](/docs/recipes/playdeck-video/)
